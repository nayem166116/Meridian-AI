About Meridian

Building the workspace for the agent-run company

We started Meridian because we believe every team will soon run part of its work through AI agents - and they'll need a place to build, approve, and trust them.

Meridian AI was founded in 2023 by a small team of engineers and designers who had spent years building internal automation tools at larger tech companies. We kept hitting the same wall: automation was powerful, but brittle and invisible. When something broke, nobody knew why. When something needed a human decision, there was no clean way to pause and ask.

So we set out to build something different - a workspace where AI agents are first-class citizens: versioned, observable, and accountable to the humans who rely on them. Today, Meridian powers agents that qualify leads, triage support tickets, reconcile invoices, and summarize meetings for teams across sales, support, operations, and finance.

What we believe

Transparency by default

Every agent action should be visible, explainable, and traceable back to a decision.

Humans stay in control

Approvals and guardrails aren't an afterthought - they're built into how agents run.

Ship fast, safely

Good tooling means teams can move quickly without cutting corners on safety.

Built with our customers

Our roadmap is shaped directly by the operators who use Meridian every day.
